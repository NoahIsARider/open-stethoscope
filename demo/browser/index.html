Heart-murmur screening entirely in your browser — a 404K-parameter model runs locally via WebAssembly. No audio leaves your machine.

1 · Input

Upload a WAV / MP3 / OGG recording (5–15 s recommended, 4 kHz target; longer clips are trimmed to the first ~8 s). The model was trained on 4 kHz phonocardiograms recorded with a digital stethoscope at the mitral valve — other positions are supported but may be less reliable.
